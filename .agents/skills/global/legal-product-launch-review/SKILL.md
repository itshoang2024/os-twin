# Legal Product Launch Review

Use this skill to prepare product launches for legal and compliance review.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive launch risks and escalation guidance.

## Intake

Confirm product, launch date, jurisdictions, users affected, data used, claims made, third-party dependencies, regulated users or industries, and destination of the review.

## Review Focus

Check privacy, consumer protection, accessibility, security, IP, marketing claims, dark patterns, minors, AI usage, payments, subscriptions, export/sanctions, platform rules, and support obligations.

## Output

Return launch risk level, blocking items, open questions, mitigation options, and named approvers. Keep subjective risk calls flagged for attorney review.
