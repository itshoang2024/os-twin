# Legal Corporate Diligence Grid

Use this skill for first-pass corporate diligence grids and issue extraction.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive issue summaries and escalation guidance.

## Inputs

Confirm transaction type, target, document set, materiality threshold, jurisdiction, diligence scope, and reviewer.

## Review Focus

Classify documents by type. Extract parties, dates, obligations, consents, change-of-control rights, exclusivity, non-competes, MFN, assignment restrictions, termination rights, liabilities, litigation, IP, employment, privacy, and regulatory issues.

## Output

Produce a diligence grid with document, issue category, source cite, materiality, required follow-up, owner, and attorney-review flag. Mark unread or partially read sources clearly.
