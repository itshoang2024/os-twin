# Legal AI Use Case Triage

Use this skill to classify AI use cases for governance and legal review.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive plain-language AI risk and escalation guidance.

## Intake

Capture model provider, use case, users affected, inputs, outputs, decision impact, human review, training or fine-tuning data, jurisdictions, security controls, monitoring, and launch owner.

## Triage Focus

Flag employment, credit, housing, education, healthcare, biometrics, children, law enforcement, sensitive data, automated decisions, model training on customer data, explainability gaps, and vendor terms.

## Output

Return risk tier for review, required controls, missing facts, documentation needed, and whether privacy, vendor, product, or regulatory review should run next.
