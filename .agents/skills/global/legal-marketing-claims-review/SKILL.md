# Legal Marketing Claims Review

Use this skill to check marketing copy for claims that need substantiation or legal review.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive copy risks and escalation guidance.

## Review Inputs

Capture the copy, audience, channel, jurisdiction, product facts, data supporting claims, competitor references, testimonials, pricing terms, and launch date.

## Review Focus

Flag objective claims, superiority claims, savings or performance claims, testimonials, endorsements, environmental claims, AI claims, medical or regulated claims, pricing disclosures, scarcity language, and ambiguous guarantees.

## Output

Produce a table with original copy, issue, substantiation needed, safer alternative, and attorney-review flag. Do not certify a claim as compliant.
