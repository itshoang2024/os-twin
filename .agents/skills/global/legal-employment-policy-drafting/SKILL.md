# Legal Employment Policy Drafting

Use this skill to prepare employment policy drafts and review notes.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive policy questions and escalation guidance.

## Inputs

Confirm workforce jurisdictions, employee types, policy purpose, current policy, required notices, union or works council considerations, and reviewer.

## Drafting Focus

Check consistency with company practice, local law variation, protected rights, leave and accommodation, wage-hour rules, privacy monitoring, reporting channels, anti-retaliation, and enforcement process.

## Output

Produce draft language, jurisdiction notes, implementation checklist, and attorney-review flags. Do not represent the policy as compliant in every jurisdiction.
