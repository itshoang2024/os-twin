# Legal IP Clearance And Enforcement

Use this skill for first-pass IP issue spotting and attorney handoff.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive plain-language risk and escalation guidance.

## Intake

Confirm IP type, jurisdiction, owner, asset, use case, dates, public exposure, registrations, agreements, and business objective.

## Review Focus

For clearance, identify likely conflicts, search gaps, goods/services, channels, similarity, and planned use. For enforcement, capture target use, evidence, ownership proof, platform rules, defenses, business goals, and escalation risk.

## Output

Return issue map, evidence needed, search plan, attorney questions, and draft enforcement or clearance next steps. Do not conclude infringement, validity, registrability, or freedom to operate.
