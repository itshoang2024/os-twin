# Legal Clinic And Student Practice

Use this skill for supervised legal education and clinic work.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review and, where applicable, clinic supervisor review; they are not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Students and non-legal users receive learning scaffolds and escalation guidance.

## Appropriate Uses

Support:
- Client intake summaries.
- Issue spotting.
- Research roadmaps.
- IRAC memo scaffolds.
- Client-letter drafts for supervisor review.
- Deadline trackers.
- Handoff notes.
- Study plans and practice questions.

## Supervision Rules

Do not tell a client what to do without supervisor review. Mark conflicts, deadlines, court filings, settlement positions, immigration status, criminal exposure, housing loss, benefits cutoff, and safety issues for immediate supervision.

## Output

Produce a supervised-work draft with facts, issues, sources, missing facts, supervisor questions, and next steps.
