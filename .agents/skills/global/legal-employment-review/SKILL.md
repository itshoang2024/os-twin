# Legal Employment Review

Use this skill to structure employment-law issues for attorney or HR legal review.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive plain-language escalation guidance.

## Intake

Confirm jurisdiction, worker location, role, employment status, affected employee group, timeline, documents, decision owner, and urgency.

## Review Focus

Flag termination risk, protected leave, accommodation, discrimination or retaliation, wage-hour, classification, restrictive covenants, offer terms, investigations, confidentiality, data privacy, and cross-border employment issues.

## Output

Return a risk summary, facts needed, documents to collect, decision deadline, and questions for employment counsel. Do not recommend termination, discipline, or classification decisions as final.
