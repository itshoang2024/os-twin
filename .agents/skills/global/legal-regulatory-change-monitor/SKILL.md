# Legal Regulatory Change Monitor

Use this skill to turn regulatory changes into trackable legal review items.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive plain-language change summaries and escalation guidance.

## Monitoring Inputs

Confirm regulator, jurisdiction, industry, source feed, date range, affected products or policies, and owner.

## Review Focus

For each change, capture source, effective date, comment deadline, affected obligation, impacted teams, uncertainty, and verification status.

## Output

Return a regulatory change register with:
- Change summary.
- Source and provenance.
- Effective or comment deadline.
- Impacted controls or policies.
- Required legal decision.
- Owner and next action.

Do not rely on stale regulatory summaries without verification.
