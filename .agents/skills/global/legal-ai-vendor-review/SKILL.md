# Legal AI Vendor Review

Use this skill for AI vendor and model provider contract review.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive vendor-risk summaries and escalation guidance.

## Review Inputs

Confirm service, data submitted, output use, training or retention rights, model change controls, subprocessors, security commitments, user base, regulated use, and business owner.

## Review Focus

Check:
- Customer data training and retention.
- Confidentiality and data segregation.
- Output ownership and restrictions.
- IP indemnity and third-party claims.
- Accuracy disclaimers and warranty gaps.
- Model changes, suspension, and support.
- Audit, security, incident notice, and compliance documentation.

## Output

Produce issues, fallback asks, required approvals, and open questions. Do not approve AI vendor use without counsel and security review.
