# Legal Litigation Response Triage

Use this skill to route dispute-related material to the right legal response.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive escalation instructions and preservation reminders.

## Intake

Capture document type, received date, sender, parties, jurisdiction, response deadline, service method, affected custodians, preservation needs, and responsible attorney.

## Triage Focus

Flag litigation hold needs, subpoena scope, demand response deadline, insurance notice, regulator involvement, confidentiality, privilege, discovery restrictions, public statements, and settlement communications.

## Output

Return a triage memo with deadline source, preservation actions, immediate risks, documents to collect, owner, and attorney questions. Do not draft a final legal response without counsel direction.
