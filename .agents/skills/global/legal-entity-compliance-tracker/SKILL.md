# Legal Entity Compliance Tracker

Use this skill to organize entity maintenance and filing obligations.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive operational tracking and escalation guidance.

## Inputs

Capture entity name, jurisdiction, entity type, registration numbers, registered agent, filing calendar source, owner, and evidence of prior filings.

## Tracking Focus

Track annual reports, taxes, beneficial ownership, licenses, minutes, consents, foreign qualifications, registered agent renewals, and status checks.

## Output

Produce a compliance register with obligation, jurisdiction, due date, source, status, owner, evidence link, and attorney-review flag. Mark dates as unverified unless checked against the current official source.
