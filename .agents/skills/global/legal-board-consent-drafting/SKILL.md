# Legal Board Consent Drafting

Use this skill to prepare draft corporate approvals for attorney review.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive an authorization checklist and escalation guidance.

## Inputs

Confirm entity, jurisdiction, governing documents, action to approve, required approval body, signers, effective date, related documents, and precedent form.

## Drafting Focus

Check authority, recitals, resolutions, officer authorization, signature blocks, attachments, conflicts, filing requirements, and whether shareholder or manager approval may also be needed.

## Output

Produce a draft package with assumptions, missing governance documents, approval checklist, and attorney-review flags. Do not state that the approval is legally effective without counsel review.
