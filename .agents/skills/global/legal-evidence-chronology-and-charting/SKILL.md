# Legal Evidence Chronology And Charting

Use this skill to organize evidence into attorney-reviewable timelines and proof matrices.

## Mandatory Legal Gate

Apply `legal-common` first. Outputs are drafts for qualified attorney review, not legal advice or final legal conclusions. Non-legal users receive fact organization and escalation guidance.

## Inputs

Confirm matter, jurisdiction, claim or issue, side, phase, evidence set, source restrictions, and reviewer.

## Charting Rules

- Every row needs a source cite or `[needs source]`.
- Separate fact, inference, and legal conclusion.
- Mark privilege, confidentiality, and discovery-use restrictions.
- Preserve contradictory evidence.
- Do not close evidence gaps by extrapolation.

## Output

Produce a chronology or proof chart with date, fact, source, issue, supports, cuts against, verification status, and open questions. Include a gap list for counsel.
