# Legal Output Checklist

Before sending a legal output, confirm:

- User role is known.
- Jurisdiction or forum is known or explicitly assumed.
- Company, practice, and matter profile status is stated: loaded, absent, or assumed.
- Source set and read coverage are stated.
- All legal authorities have provenance tags.
- Missing citations, deadlines, or legal tests are marked for verification.
- Privilege/confidentiality posture matches the intended destination.
- OSTwin role routing is appropriate: QA for acceptance review, audit for compliance/risk validation, staff-manager for critical meta-review.
- Non-legal users receive escalation guidance instead of legal conclusions.
- Output states it is a draft for qualified attorney review.
