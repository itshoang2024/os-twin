# Citation Verification Log Template

Path: `~/.ostwin/legal-profiles/verification-log.md`

Use one entry per verified legal fact or citation.

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD

- Fact or citation:
- Verified by:
- Jurisdiction or forum:
- Source type: statute | regulation | case | agency guidance | contract | policy | filing | other
- Source checked:
- Pin cite, section, clause, or page:
- Source date or effective date:
- Result: confirmed | corrected | could not verify
- Notes:
```

Do not mark a proposition verified unless the source was actually checked.
