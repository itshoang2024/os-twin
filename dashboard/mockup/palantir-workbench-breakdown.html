Reference states included

This rewrite condenses the five screenshots into a single FE-readable anatomy: Selection, Histogram, Layers styling, Production Line exploration, and Template authoring all share one shell contract.

UX rules

  • Left drawer is mode-specific context.
  • Center graph remains the primary work surface.
  • Right inspector is the single editing home.
  • Bottom rail is reserved for series and time context.

FE mapping

  • WorkbenchShell owns layout.
  • GraphCanvas owns nodes and edges.
  • SelectionInspector owns contextual editing.
  • HistogramPanel and LayersStylingPanel become drawer modes.
