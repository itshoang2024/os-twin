Internal Audit Risk Platform - Multi-Industry Feature Map (CPO Plan v2)

CPO re-architecture of the junior plan: an industry-neutral platform spine plus pluggable vertical packs (Financial Services flagship; Technology & SaaS, Retail, Public Sector). Synthesized with the internal audit platform architecture slide: four-tier delivery, operational lifecycle, domain analytics, 8+8 agents, and ontology-first development. See the Strategy tab. Prepared 2026-06-01.
Product thesis
One industry-neutral risk operating system. The spine is horizontal; industry knowledge ships as pluggable packs, never hardcoded.
Platform spine
Sources, infrastructure, governance, Smart Table, ontology, workflow, and experience are shared by every industry.
Pack platform (the moat)
A Pack SDK, registry, Configuration Studio, and curated regulatory content turn a new vertical into configuration, not a rebuild.
Flagship then proof
Prove the engine on Financial Services in P1, then ship Technology and SaaS via the SDK in P3 to prove verticalization.
Ontology development
Ontology is a living product: authored, reviewed, versioned, migrated, observed, and improved from audit feedback.
